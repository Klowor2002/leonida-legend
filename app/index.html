The roadmap — eight phases to the platinum

The sequence is the strategy. Each phase unlocks the next one's map, money or tools, and the counters that look impossible from the start screen — 250 stunts, 100 WAiNK spots, $250,000 laundered — are only impossible if you start them at the end.

Sources & honesty

Leonida Legend is an unofficial fan work. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list; every guide, strategy, the roadmap, the rules and the FAQ are original writing for this guide, and each card names its sources beside the guide it supports. Grand Theft Auto, GTA VI, Vice City and Leonida belong to Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. Where this page and the shipped game disagree, the game wins — the guide says so in its own rules.
