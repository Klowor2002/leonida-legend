    Sources & honesty

    Official list basis. The 55 trophy names, their official descriptions and their grades (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze) are reproduced from the official PlayStation 5 trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI. Names, descriptions and grades are quoted exactly as published; everything else on this page — every guide, tip, difficulty rating and time estimate — was written for this guide.

    Per-card basis. Each trophy card carries a Basis line naming what its guide rests on: the official list entry, the kind of content it belongs to (a story mission, a stranger strand, an activity, a robbery system), and — where a place or system is named — the setting facts Rockstar has shown publicly.

    Setting facts. What this guide says about Leonida itself — Vice City and Ocean Beach, VCIA (the international airport), the duo of Lucia and Jason, the state's gangs, swamps and keys — comes from Rockstar's official trailers and official game pages, not from leaks or rumour.

    The honesty note. GTA VI launches on 26 May 2026. Until the game is in players' hands, exact in-game labels, markers and locations cannot be verified against the shipped game. Where a guide describes a trigger that only the shipped game can settle, the card says so, and once the game is out, those details are confirmed against it — and where this guide and the in-game menu differ, the in-game menu wins. No guide here invents a precise location the game has not shown.

    Unofficial fan guide. Leonida Legend is an unofficial, fan-made companion. All guide text and artwork on this page are original to it; nothing here is copied from Rockstar.
