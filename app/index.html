    Sources, and an honest note

    What is official. The trophy names, the official descriptions and the grades — one platinum, two gold, six silver, forty-six bronze — are quoted exactly from the official PlayStation 5 trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI. Quotation is all they are: the words are the list's own.

    What is ours. Everything else. Each card's guide, tip, difficulty score, time estimate and phase were written for Leonida Legend, and each card ends with a Basis line saying what its guide leans on — the official entry, the family of content it belongs to, and the setting facts Rockstar has made public in trailers and on its official pages (Vice City and Ocean Beach, VCIA airport, the two protagonists, the gangs and wetlands of Leonida).

    The pre-launch note. GTA VI releases on 26 May 2026. Until the shipped game can be checked, exact in-game labels, markers and locations in these guides are careful inferences from the official list, not verified sightings — no guide here states a precise location the game has not shown. Once the game is out, details get confirmed against it, and wherever this page and the in-game menu disagree, the in-game menu is right.
